# Web app

[kaja.io](https://kaja.io) is where your cloud account lives. You don't chat there: you link Telegram, make
widgets, run sandboxes and see your usage.

## Signing in

There are no passwords. Sign up or in with your email, then type the 6-digit code Kaja emails you (it works
for 10 minutes), or use Google where offered. New accounts are made only on the sign-up page, which first asks
you to confirm you're 18 or over, accept the [Terms](/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy), and consent to Kaja
processing any health information you choose to share. Your name and picture come from your public
[Gravatar](https://gravatar.com) profile, or from Google, and you can change them on your profile page.

The terminal's [device login](/start/modes#cloud-mode) lands here too: open
[kaja.io/device](https://kaja.io/device), sign in, and confirm the code from your terminal.

## Pages

The top menu has one item per section, and a section's pages are tabs inside it.

| Page                     | What it's for                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Dashboard › Overview** | where you're signed in, the **Connect Telegram** card and a sandbox summary                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Dashboard › Stats**    | your usage: sessions, messages, tokens and latency by day, channel, tool, persona and model                                                                                                                                    |
| **Agent › Widget**       | create, edit, link, publish and revoke [widgets](/use/widget)                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Agent › Sandbox**      | your sandbox key and the `docker run` command for a [sandbox](/use/sandbox) on your own machine, the sandboxes you run, and whether others may use yours and you theirs (whoever runs a shared one can see the pages it opens) |
| **Profile**              | your name and avatar, email (a change takes a code from your current address, then one from the new one), and deleting your account with everything stored under it                                                            |

The language picker at the bottom of every page switches the site. Signed in, it also saves to your
account, so emails, the Telegram bot and the terminal's cloud mode follow.

There's no page for abilities or keys: every persona and ability is yours already, and Kaja provides the API
keys they need (see [Abilities in the cloud](/abilities/marketplace#in-the-cloud)).
